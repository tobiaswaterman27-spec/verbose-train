Wildhollow · design bible · draft three

The Fellaron Register

Stripped back to what has actually been decided. The story is not written yet — the blanks below are deliberate and stay blank until they are filled in properly.

I

Decided

Everything not on that list is open. An earlier draft carried a premise about drained Wellsprings and a headmaster forcing growth, a faculty, and a class of ten. That was invented rather than agreed, and has been removed. It is in the git history if any of it is ever wanted back.

II

The story

Not written. To be told, not invented.

What has to land here, roughly in this order: what is wrong in the world, why the player leaves the school, who the two friends are and what they want, what the player is trying to do, and how it ends.

III

The ten elements

Inherited, not confirmed. The three built Fellarons use Verdant, Ember and Tide, so the wheel is load-bearing for what already exists — but the count, the names and the regions are all still open.

IV

The hundred

The standard

The first attempt at this roster produced eight Fellarons in an afternoon and seven were thrown away. They failed for the same three reasons every time: the body plan was one already on the roster, the element showed up as a colour instead of as a shape, and there was no answer to what the animal did when nobody was looking.

Nothing joins the register until all six of these are written down, in words, before any geometry is placed.

  1. A body plan no other Fellaron uses. Not a variation. A different animal.
  2. A living. What it eats, where it sleeps, what it is afraid of.
  3. Its element in its shape. Colour is the last five per cent, never the first.
  4. Three things you could learn by watching it. If there is no answer, it is not finished.
  5. A reason it changes that is the thing it already does.
  6. One fact with no gameplay purpose whatsoever.

And one rule about process: one at a time. The seven that were cut were designed in a batch, and a batch converges — the second borrows from the first, and the eighth is a recolour of the third.

V

Built so far

Three of one hundred. Sprite models exist and validate in all four views. Written out to the standard above as the worked example of it.
