# KITHBOUND

> *A land of wild spirits, a fading doorway, and a kid who still knows how to listen.*

A creature-bonding RPG set in the sunlit land of **Ellumen**, where wild spirits called **Kith** are born out of places — and the doorway they cross through is starting to thin.

You don't catch Kith. You earn them, one Rite at a time, and you can lose them if you stop being worth their trust.

**Genre:** Creature-bonding RPG — exploration, turn-based tactical battles, party building
**Platform:** Browser (Vite + TypeScript, HTML5 Canvas)
**Status:** 🌱 Concept — design locked, implementation not started

---

## What makes it its own thing

- **Rites, not capture.** Every Kith species has its own short challenge that reflects its nature. Fail it and that species gets warier.
- **Trust you can lose.** Kith that are overworked, benched forever, or repeatedly knocked out will lose faith — and can walk away for good.
- **Growth shaped by play.** No level-30 evolutions. Kith mature toward whatever you actually use them for, so the same starter becomes a different creature for every player.
- **Six Aspects, not eighteen types.** Ember · Gale · Gloom · Tide · Stone · Lumen — a clean ring where each beats two and loses to two.
- **3v3 battles with rows.** Tight, fast, positional.
